BOAT HANDLING · CONNECTED MARINA
Docking Trainer 3D
Practice freely, or take on docking challenges with fixed conditions, a destination, a timer and contact penalties.

 

 
Choose a race
Red: 1 second. Yellow: 0.5 seconds. Go on green. Early throttle adds one 5-second penalty. Contacts add no time. Finish stern-in with four lines.

 
Choose a challenge
Docking score = elapsed time + contact penalties. Select a challenge to start immediately with its fixed boat and conditions.

 
Instructions

 
Throttle / shift
Hold 10 FWD or REV for a gentle docking pulse; 25 gives a stronger burst. Release to return to neutral. Twin-engine boats have separate port/starboard controls and a Both column in portrait mode. The bowrider uses one engine. The workboat uses 50% and 100% forward/reverse power.

 
Steering and bow thruster
Use the steering slider and Centre button. Steering stays where you leave it, except on the workboat, where it springs to centre on release. Outboards and sterndrives turn their thrust with steering. Fixed-shaft inboards steer forward propwash with rudders; reverse thrust stays along the keel. On the motor yacht, hold the bow thruster buttons for sideways bow corrections.

 
Docking lines
Tap a port or starboard line near a dock cleat to attach it; tap again to release. Bow and stern lines restrain their respective ends. Spring lines resist fore-and-aft movement and help pivot off a dock. Lines pull only when taut. For Med mooring, deploy the anchor and attach the port and starboard Stern lines separately.

 
Fenders and contact
Deploy fenders on either side to cushion side contact and help the hull slide along a dock. Contact messages show severity and impact speed. In docking levels, gentle, firm, hard and crash contacts add 2, 10, 30 and 60 seconds. Fenders reduce measured impact severity. The workboat race has no contact penalties.

 
Anchor
Drop/pay sets the anchor below the bow and lets rode pay out as you move away. Hold fixes the rode length. Retrieve reels it in and pulls toward the anchor until it comes aboard. In Precision Anchor Drop, the anchor itself must be inside the orange zone.

 
Camera and map
Cycle Far, Medium and Helm views. Drag to look, then use Recenter to face forward. Lock view, below the port fender button, holds the camera position and angle relative to the map. It automatically releases when the boat leaves the screen. Recenter or changing views also releases it. Settings adjusts camera catch-up and helm height. Tap the north-up mini-map to enlarge it. Gold marks the target; orange marks an anchor-drop zone and the moving boat.

 
Moving traffic
Practice offers None, Medium (up to five boats) and High (up to ten boats). Traffic follows ten fixed routes and holds course while underway, even if you are in the way. Boats wait about 50 feet off an occupied destination, then enter when it clears. Traffic berths stay free of parked boats.

 
Completing levels
Arrivals require two lines, alignment with the berth and speed below the briefing limit for three seconds. Med arrivals require both stern lines and an anchor. Departures finish when you enter the exit zone with every line released and the anchor stowed; no speed limit or waiting period applies. Races require a stern-in finish in the marked slip, aligned within 10°. It finishes immediately when both bow lines and both stern lines are attached to the four pilings of the marked destination slip.
